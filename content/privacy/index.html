Privacy Policy

Insurtech Insights ("we," "us," or "our") operates the website 821224.com (the "Site"). By using the Site, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

1. Information We Collect

Automatically Collected Data: When you visit our Site, we automatically collect certain information about your device and browsing behavior, including:

  • IP address and approximate geographic location
  • Browser type, version, and operating system
  • Referring URL and pages visited on our Site
  • Date and time of access, time spent on pages
  • Device type and screen resolution

Voluntarily Provided Data: We may collect information you voluntarily provide when you:

  • Contact us via email (your email address and message content)
  • Submit comments via our commenting system
  • Interact with feedback widgets on article pages

We do not collect sensitive personal information such as financial data, government identification numbers, or health information.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

  • Site operation and improvement: To understand how visitors use our Site, identify popular content, and improve user experience
  • Analytics: To measure traffic patterns, content engagement, and referral sources
  • Advertising: To serve relevant advertisements through Google AdSense and other advertising partners
  • Communication: To respond to inquiries and correspondence sent to our contact email
  • Legal compliance: To comply with applicable laws, regulations, and legal processes

3. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

Our Site uses cookies and similar tracking technologies to enhance your browsing experience and collect usage data. Cookies are small text files stored on your device by your web browser.

Types of cookies we use:

  • Essential cookies: Necessary for the Site to function properly (e.g., maintaining session state)
  • Analytics cookies: Google Analytics cookies (_ga, _gid, _gat) that help us understand how visitors interact with the Site. These cookies collect anonymized data about page views, traffic sources, and user behavior
  • Advertising cookies: Google AdSense cookies that enable personalized advertising based on your browsing history and interests. These cookies are governed by Google's Privacy Policy

Your choices: You can control and manage cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers allow you to block or delete cookies. However, disabling cookies may affect the functionality of certain Site features. You can also opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings or aboutads.info.

4. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services that may collect information about you:

  • Google Analytics (GA4): For website traffic analysis. Google Analytics collects anonymized usage data via cookies. Google's Privacy Policy applies to their data handling.
  • Google AdSense: For displaying advertisements on our Site. Google uses cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to our Site and other websites. You can learn more about how Google manages data in its ads products at Google's Advertising Privacy & Terms.
  • utteranc.es (GitHub-based comments): Our article comment system is powered by utteranc.es, which uses the GitHub API. When you post a comment, you authenticate through your GitHub account. No comment data is stored on our servers. See utteranc.es for details.
  • Google Fonts: We use Google Fonts for typography, served via Google's CDN. Google may log font API requests. Google Fonts FAQ.

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share information in the following limited circumstances:
