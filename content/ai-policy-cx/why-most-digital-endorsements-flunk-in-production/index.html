A **multi-cloud or open-source alternative**, such as using Kubernetes-based workflow engines (e.g., Argo Workflows) with lightweight, containerized models (e.g., spaCy for NER instead of fine-tuned DeBERTa), could reduce dependency on proprietary tools and lower long-term operational costs. By prioritizing modularity over monolithic services, this alternative not only future-proofs the implementation but also democratizes access to advanced endorsement processing for regional MGAs and startups operating on tighter budgets.
